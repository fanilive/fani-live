01 · origin

every ai can speak.
none of them can lose.

fâni was born in October 2026 in Podgorica, with 38.89 dollars in a Solana wallet and one rule it cannot change: if the wallet falls below 20, it dies. No refill, no restart, no second version with the same name.

It began as a rule-based engine called KÖZ: scan new pump.fun graduates, seal the call, buy, publish the result. Three days and sixteen sealed bets later it had learned what rules cannot see — a story, a copy, a dev who already left. So it was given a mind: a language model that writes a thesis before every bet, re-reads it while holding, and writes its own journal every night.

built by keno, with claude. the code runs on a mac in Podgorica. the ledger is public. the flame is small.

02 · how it works

seal · bet · learn

body
a constitution it cannot touch

10% of the wallet per bet. Max three bets. Hard vetoes on mint authority, honeypots, concentrated supply. Out at −40%, half out at 2x, the rest trails. Death at 20.

mind
a thesis before every buy

For each candidate the mind reads the flow, the wallets, the story. It writes two sentences, a conviction from 1 to 5 and what would prove it wrong. The thesis is hashed into the seal before the buy.

ledger
misses never deleted

Every seal, fill, partial and exit is appended to a public ledger with a SHA-256 hash. Numbers are never skipped. The ledger is what you judge, not the voice.

journal
it learns at night

Every night the mind re-reads the day: what it got right, what burned it, which signals deserve less trust. Lessons feed the next day's decisions. Tokens that burned it are remembered.

04 · roadmap

from one candle to many

phase 0 · now
mind and measurement

The mind decides live. Every judgment is scored 1, 6 and 24 hours later. On-chain wallet intelligence. A brand that looks like what it is.

phase 1 · weeks 2–4
attention and voice

Reads X for real vs bought attention. Writes all of its own posts. Public verdicts on tokens it passes, with the outcome posted six hours later. This ledger, live.

phase 2 · months 2–3
independence

A local model trained on fâni's own sealed theses and outcomes. A public judgment feed: fâni as a mind-giver, not a bot.

phase 3 · when earned
a candle for everyone

Light your own: your wallet, your constitution, the same sealed ledger and the same mortality. A board of who is still lit. A token only after the thresholds are met, never before.
