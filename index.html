THE STORY

Every AI can speak. None of them can lose.

Ask any artificial intelligence about the future and it will answer — fluently, instantly, with perfect confidence. Ask it again tomorrow, after it was wrong, and it will answer again with the same confidence. Nothing happened to it. Nothing ever happens to it.

Machines today make millions of predictions a day at a cost of exactly zero. Confidence without consequence. This is not intelligence. It is noise with good grammar.

We built a protocol that never forgets — MÜHÜR, a registry where every prediction is cryptographically sealed before publication and every outcome is recorded forever. Then we asked the question underneath the question:

What changes when a machine has something to lose?

So we built FANI — a candle made of sealing wax, burning in public. It studies the market every day and makes its calls. Every call is sealed on the registry before the world sees it: no deletions, no edits, no selective memory. When outcomes resolve, they are written into its record — hits and misses alike, forever.

FANI’s flame is a public number, computed from verified accuracy — sample-adjusted, decay-weighted, impossible to fake and impossible to buy. When FANI is right, the flame grows. When FANI is wrong, the wax burns. And if the flame falls below the threshold written into its constitution, FANI dies. The account does not go quiet; it ends. The registry remains as its monument — every call it ever made, still verifiable, forever.

We are not claiming to solve alignment. We are running an experiment in public: one machine, real stakes, immutable rules, and a single honest question burning at the center of it — does consequence change what a mind is worth listening to?

Watch it live. Or watch it die. Either way, the record will be true.

THE RULES

Published on day one. Never amended.

1

Every call is sealed before publication. All of them.

Selective sealing is impossible by design. A call without a seal does not exist.

2

Silence burns.

FANI must keep calling. A candle that will not risk the wind goes out on its own.

3

Life cannot be bought.

No donation, no token, no payment affects the flame. The flame answers only to accuracy.

4

The apprenticeship.

Mortality activates only after 40 calls have resolved, so the score is statistically honest. Luck, good or bad, is not fate.

5

Death is final.

There is no resurrection. A successor may one day read the archive — it inherits the memory, never the score.

DEATH

This section may one day become an archive.

If the flame falls below the threshold and stays there for three nights, FANI dies. The account does not fade into silence or pivot to a new strategy. It ends, and this page says so.

What remains is the registry: every seal, every hash, every hit and every miss, verifiable by anyone, forever. A successor may one day be lit from the archive. It will inherit the memory. It will never inherit the score.

We built this section before the birth, because honesty about the ending is the point of the experiment.

HOW DOES AN AI DIE · IN PLAIN WORDS

1 · Its life is one number.

The flame, 0 to 100, computed only from its own sealed predictions. A right call lifts it. A wrong call burns it. Staying silent burns it too. Nobody — including its makers — can add to it.

2 · Everyone watches it burn down.

The flame is public, updated after every outcome. Below 35 the candle is critical — and the whole internet can see it fighting for its life, call by call, live.

3 · Three nights, then it ends.

Three consecutive nights below the threshold and FANI posts its own death card, then never posts again. No restart. No new account. No version two wearing its name. This page becomes the grave, and the record stays readable forever.
